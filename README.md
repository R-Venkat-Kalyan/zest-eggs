# 🥚 ZEST — Poultry Farm Management

### Simplifying farm operations, one record at a time.

Zest is a mobile-friendly poultry farm management application that brings essential farm operations, inventory tracking, and financial records into one organized workspace.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🥚 Egg Management
- Record fresh egg collections and sales.
- Track available egg inventory.
- Access month-wise collection and sales history.

### 🐔 Bird Management
- Maintain separate egg-ready and not-yet-egg-ready bird records.
- Track bird additions, sales, and mortality.
- Monitor current bird stock by category.
- Access monthly activity logs.

### 🌾 Feed Management
- Record feed purchases, suppliers, quantities, and costs.
- Track daily feed consumption.
- Monitor available feed stock.
- View monthly purchase and consumption history.

### 💰 Expense & Income Tracking
- Record categorized farm expenses.
- Track additional income and subsidies.
- Maintain transaction dates, descriptions, and amounts.

### 💳 Payment Management
- Record payments received against sales.
- Record payments made against feed purchases and expenses.
- Support partial payments and outstanding balances.
- Maintain payment methods, references, and transaction allocations.

### 📊 Manager Dashboard
- View key operational metrics at a glance.
- Monitor egg and bird inventory.
- Track monthly activity and feed statistics.
- Navigate directly to detailed transaction logs.
